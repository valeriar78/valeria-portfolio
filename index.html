About

Creative Director & Design Leader with 25+ years shaping international womenswear and menswear brands — leading teams of up to 10 people, repositioning brand identity across a network of 350+ points of sale, and driving double-digit e-commerce growth.

My path has spanned Miss Sixty, Tally Weijl and Doppelgänger, scaling retail turnover to €400M, launching NYFW capsules, and building global partnerships with Renault and Harrods.

Approach

I combine creative vision, sustainable sourcing and commercial leadership into a single, coherent direction — connecting product, brand and retail from mood board to store shelf. Along the way, I oversee every phase personally, from international textile scouting to final retail execution.
